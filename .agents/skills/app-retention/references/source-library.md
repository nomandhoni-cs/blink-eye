# Source library

Select sources by ID and context. For the chapter map and claim corrections, see [video and evidence](video-and-evidence.md). The [source index](../assets/research-source-index.csv) includes additional product and accessibility sources, plus scope limits.

**How to read this:** *Named* means the speaker identifies the person, book, study topic, or report category. *Matched* means the best public source for a recognizable claim, not proof the creator personally used it. *Supplementary* is research added to inform design or evaluation. This covers the retrieved spoken topics, the publicly traceable claims, and the related episodes. The video description does not provide a paper list. This is a trace of the captions and public references, not a claim to have verified unspoken on-screen credits or to have obtained every full text. Links lead to publisher/DOI records, full text where available, or reputable public reporting. A DOI alone may be an abstract or require access. For a sortable index with source limitations and additional product links, use [research-source-index.csv](../assets/research-source-index.csv).
